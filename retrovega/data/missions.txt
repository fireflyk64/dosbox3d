# Edit freely: tools/rvdata.py checks every block.
#
# The rules every mission flies by (a mission's own `rules` line overrides): what a bolt must do to hit a ship
# (hit=ships: each type's ships.csv choice; sphere: within its radius, the old game's volume; box: a box of the
# sphere's volume in the ship's frame, grown tall for the thin ships; silhouette: through its silhouettes), how the
# AI shoots (aim=cone: the old game's, no lead and every ready gun within the weapon's cone; lead: at the lead
# point, scattered a little), whether the ship a player has selected holds its guns while that player is within 37
# degrees ahead of it (hold=on, the old game's), and what a collision costs (ram=old: the square of the closing
# speed in km a step, halved, the old game's; soft: a dozen points head on)
#
# A ship line's turrets=off keeps a capital ship's turrets silent: the home carrier with enemies at nav 0 has it, or
# its turrets would have those fighters dead in seconds, with the player only watching (measured 2026-10-31)
rules    hit=sphere aim=cone hold=on ram=old
